AI Prompts to Complete Your Audit

Use During Audit Prep 
PROMPT: Score a dimension
I'm completing an AI readiness audit for [CLIENT NAME], a [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]. Here are my notes from their [DIMENSION NAME] process: [PASTE YOUR NOTES]. Score this dimension from 1–10 for AI readiness using these criteria: 1–3 = fully manual, no tools; 4–6 = some tools but not integrated; 7–9 = automated with AI involvement; 10 = fully systemized with AI throughout. Give me: the score, one sentence explaining why, and one specific AI opportunity they're missing.
PROMPT: Write a dimension finding
Based on these discovery notes for [CLIENT NAME]'s [DIMENSION]: [PASTE NOTES]. Write a finding in this format: Current state (2 sentences) → What's working (1 sentence) → Key gap (1 sentence) → AI opportunity (2 sentences with a specific tool or automation). Keep it professional and direct — this will be shown to the client.
PROMPT: Write a prioritized recommendation
Based on this finding for [CLIENT NAME]: [PASTE FINDING]. Write a prioritized recommendation that includes: what to do specifically, why it's the priority, what tools they'll need, realistic time to implement, and what outcome to measure. Format it as a structured card — not a paragraph. Make it immediately actionable — the client should be able to start this without needing to ask a follow-up question.
PROMPT: Write the executive summary
I've completed an AI readiness audit for [CLIENT NAME]. Their score: [TOTAL]/60. Key findings by dimension: [PASTE SCORES + ONE-LINE FINDING PER DIMENSION]. Top 3 recommendations: [LIST]. Write a 4-sentence executive summary: business context, overall score interpretation, top 3 opportunities in one sentence each, highest-priority first action. Tone: confident, specific, direct. No filler language.
